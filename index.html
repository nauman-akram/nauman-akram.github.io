Profile

Three years of professional experience, six since first working in data science — across commercial delivery and academic research.

I'm an AI Software Developer working across computer vision, edge AI, data engineering and automation, with Python as my primary language throughout my career. My work sits where research meets production: taking approaches that exist in papers and making them run reliably on real hardware, at scale, for real businesses.

Currently I lead development of a real-time video analytics platform for a major UK supermarket chain, running object detection on NVIDIA Orin edge devices deployed across retail sites nationwide — optimising models to work within tight memory and compute constraints.

Previously, as a Data Scientist, I delivered projects for commercial customers: unifying customer data across PoS and e-commerce platforms, building ingestion pipelines and change-data-capture flows on AWS, forecasting demand, and designing dashboards that directly informed business decisions. Before that I spent two years freelancing in computer vision and NLP for international clients.

My Master's thesis on enhancing Automatic Licence Plate Recognition was published at ICRITO'2025 and recognised as a Top Project at the University of Bolton.

I'm currently deepening my work in Generative AI, LLMs and RAG systems through NVIDIA and Udemy coursework, and building a RAG-based retrieval system independently.

Proposed a novel "usual route identification" algorithm and a character-wise accuracy metric for evaluating Automatic Licence Plate Recognition systems, achieving 96% evaluation accuracy versus 88% with conventional string matching — reducing false penalisation for visually similar character misreadings.
